The BOX — how a social post becomes a trusted trend

Everything flows top to bottom. Stages 1–6 get clean, counted data; stage 7 — the gate — is the honest check that decides what’s real. The hard part isn’t collecting posts; it’s that last step.

◆ / tag = built or fixed in this engagement.
STAGES 1–2
Scout & scrape
  • Company brief → search terms. An LLM turns the brief into topics + keywords to watch.
  • Apify actors fan out per query — Instagram · TikTok · Reddit · X · Google Trends · YouTubeYouTube added
  • Deeper history. YouTube reaches years back and pulled the most data of any source.
Platform history: Instagram & Reddit are recent-only. TikTok, YouTube & Google Trends go back in time — so we can actually see a rise, not just a snapshot.
▼
STAGES 3–5
Clean, extract & count
  • Ingestion. Normalize every platform’s messy data into one signal shape.
  • Entity extraction (LLM). Pull out the things people talk about — an ingredient, a brand.
  • Canonicalization. Merge variants (gummy/gummies, Athletic Brewing / Co) so signal doesn’t split — while keeping “gummy bears” distinct.
  • Timeseries. Daily buckets per entity — mentions + unique authors — zero-filled so “quiet then rising” is visible.
▼
STAGE 6
Flag rising
  • Growth state machine flags candidates whose mentions are picking up (emerging / confirmed / peaking / resurgent).
▼
Stage 7 · The Gate Is this actually a trend, or just noise?
A flagged “rising” thing must clear all three checks before it reaches the radar. This is the piece built in this engagement.
1 · Rising?
Compared against its own history — quiet before, busy now. Not just steady-and-popular.
Significance test
2 · Broad?
Many different people across several platforms — not one loud account posting ten times.
Source-breadth test
3 · Specific?
A real trackable trend, not a generic word. “Coffee”, “salt” get held; “creatine”, “ashwagandha” pass.
Specificity check
PASS → Radar
Cleared all three. Surfaces as a trend, with the real posts behind it one click away.
HOLD → kept off the radar
Failed a check. Held back — a valid, honest answer. A confirm means something because holds are the norm.
Backtest · proven on real data
Tested against your Exploding Topics benchmark: confirmed the real trends, rejected the flat everyday noise.
